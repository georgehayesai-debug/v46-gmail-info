Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. About the Application

V46 Gmail Assistant is a locally operated desktop application that connects to Gmail using Google OAuth.

2. Information Accessed

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the application may access Gmail information, including email messages and related message details, to provide its email review functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

Gmail information is used to support the application's email reading and review features. Automatic email replies are disabled in the current configuration.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

The application is designed to operate locally on the user's computer. OAuth credentials and application data may be stored locally. Data handling may also depend on services or integrations explicitly configured by the operator. Users should review those configurations before use.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Data Security

Users are responsible for securing their computer and protecting locally stored application files and credentials. Access to Google services can be revoked at any time through the user's Google Account.
